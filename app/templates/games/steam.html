How sharp steam tracking creates an edge

When sharp bettors and syndicates identify a mispriced game, heavy volume forces bookmakers to shorten (slash) their odds. Re-fetching leagues and games tracks these price changes over time in your database. Catching steam early lets you back selections before the bookmaker cuts the price further.
