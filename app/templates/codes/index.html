Why back per 1, not chance?

A short code is always likelier than a long one, so chance alone doesn't compare codes with different odds. Back per 1 staked does: at the same total odds, the code that gives back more per 1 is the likelier one, because less of the stake goes to SportyBet's margin. SportyBet keeps is the rest.
