Same odds, higher chance

The set of picks on this code's games that reaches the odds you ask for and wins most often.

How this works

At the same total odds, a code's chance depends only on how much of the bookmaker's margin its legs pile up. This looks at every pick on this code's games (in the same markets) and finds the set that reaches the odds you ask for and wins most often, at most one pick per game. Without a minimum it may lean on one longer-priced pick, because one pick carries less margin than several; set a minimum chance per leg if you'd rather every leg be likely on its own.

What rank, safer and riskier mean

Rank is by chance among the same kind of picks in that game. A safer pick does at least as well as this one in every possible result, for example "Arsenal win either half" instead of "Arsenal win both halves", or draw no bet, which refunds on a draw, instead of a straight win. An early-payout pick (1X2 2UP or 1UP) has no rank: it is compared by the results it surely wins on (its team wins) and those it may win on (its team scored enough to lead by that many).
