Why it matters: for legs on different games, a code's chance is its total odds divided into the product of the legs' returns. At the same odds, legs from markets where SportyBet keeps less give a code that wins more often. The margin changes with the odds, so it's split by zone too; a market's overall figure leans on the zones it has most picks in. These are SportyBet's own prices; a small margin is a cheaper bet, not a likelier one.
