What the numbers are. Odds are SportyBet's displayed odds. Chance is the probability SportyBet sends with each of its odds: its own estimate, with its margin taken out. It's shown only where that market's probabilities add up as they should; elsewhere it says "not given" rather than guess. Market margin is what SportyBet keeps across the whole market, worked out from the odds of every outcome alone, so it's shown only when the whole market is on offer. It's an average: SportyBet charges unlikely outcomes more than likely ones, so one pick can cost more than its market. Back per 1 is that pick's own average return, odds times SportyBet's chance, so it is only as good as SportyBet's chance. CodeMatch has no prediction of its own.
