Why bet value singles instead of accumulators?

Every leg added to an accumulator multiplies the bookmaker's house edge against you. Advantage players and syndicates bet singles on markets with minimal margin. A pick that gives back 0.96 per 1 has only a 4% house edge; combine 10 of them into a parlay and your expected return drops to 0.66 (a 34% loss). High expected return single bets minimize house drag.
