The CodeMatch button

Drag this to your browser's bookmarks bar, once:

CodeMatch {% if button_outdated %} Update needed: the bookmark in your browser is an older version. Delete it and drag this one to your bookmarks bar. {% elif button_used %} Working: it has fetched data before.{% endif %}

  1. No bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac) to show it, then drag the button onto it.
  2. Anywhere in CodeMatch, press Fetch with the CodeMatch button. SportyBet opens in a new tab.
  3. In that tab, click CodeMatch in the bookmarks bar. The data arrives here by itself; nothing to download or upload.

On a SportyBet match page with nothing queued, the button fetches that game. The first time, your browser may ask whether sportybet.com can access devices on your local network: choose Allow, since that's how it reaches this app on your computer. The button carries a secret key for this app only, so don't share it; if it stops working after moving the app to another port, drag it again from here.

Button not working? Troubleshoot here

Work through these steps in order:

  1. Re-drag the bookmark. Delete the old CodeMatch bookmark from your bar, then drag the green button above onto it again. This fixes mismatched keys or port changes.
  2. Open sportybet.com in a tab and click CodeMatch in the bar. If a black box appears bottom-right saying "asking the app…" then the connection is working.
  3. Allow local network access. Chrome may show a prompt: "sportybet.com wants to access devices on your local network." Click Allow. Without it, the button can't reach this app.
  4. If Chrome still blocks it: go to chrome://flags, search for Private Network Access, set it to Disabled, and relaunch Chrome. (This flag re-enables the old behaviour.)
  5. Clear stale fetch jobs (if the status bar shows "fetching" but nothing arrives):
  6. Check this app is running at http://127.0.0.1:5000/. The bookmark is hardcoded to that address; if you use a different port, drag a fresh bookmark from here.
